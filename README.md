# आगरी समाज · Agri Samaj

Agri Samaj is an open-source website for the Agri community. It brings local business listings, community information, publications and useful resources together in Marathi and English.

[Visit the website](https://agri-samaj-community-platform.vercel.app)

## What you can do

- Find businesses and service providers by name, category, district or business type, and view their public profiles and contact details.
- Save businesses to your account and return to them from your dashboard. Search filters can be shared as a link.
- Read about Agri history, language, culture, community organisations and initiatives.
- Find links to official scholarship, education, employment, skill development and entrepreneurship resources.
- Read published news, announcements and event information.
- Search community updates, find upcoming or past events, and download published events to your calendar.
- Browse community publications and open available PDFs.
- Send questions, corrections and suggestions through the contact form.
- Ask the AI assistant questions in Marathi or English about the website, community information and listed businesses. You can type or record a question, review its transcript, read answers with source links and listen to an AI-generated voice reply. The assistant cannot access private applications or perform account actions.

## For business owners

Create an account and submit your business for inclusion in the directory. The registration form collects owner details, business information, address, operational details and supporting documents. You can save a draft, return to finish it later and track its review status from your dashboard. Businesses appear in the public directory after administrator approval.

You can also choose an advertisement package, submit advertisement text and artwork, and provide a payment reference. Administrators review the booking and payment details manually.

## For administrators

The admin dashboard lets administrators review business applications, request changes, approve or reject listings, manage business categories, and handle advertisement bookings and payment reviews. They can also publish community articles, announcements and publications, update homepage content and contact information, and review incoming inquiries and update their status.
